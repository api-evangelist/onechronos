# OneChronos

> OneChronos operates AI-powered "Smart Market" alternative trading systems that match institutional orders using Nobel Prize-winning combinatorial auction techniques and mathematical optimization instead of traditional price-time priority. It runs US equities (a dark ATS launched in 2022), spot FX, and UK/EU equities venues, hosting periodic auctions roughly ten times per second. Subscribers connect and submit orders electronically over a modern variant of the FIX 4.2 protocol, with "expressive bidding" for custom constraints, spreads, pairs, portfolio, and hedging strategies. There is no public REST API — connectivity is FIX-based for institutional subscribers.

## Products
- [US Equities ATS](https://www.onechronos.com/products/us-equities/): Dark US equities alternative trading system.
- [FX](https://www.onechronos.com/products/fx/): Spot foreign exchange Smart Market.
- [European Equities](https://www.onechronos.com/products/european-equities/): UK and EU equities multilateral trading facilities.
